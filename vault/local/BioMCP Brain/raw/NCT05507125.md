Study Population:
  Hidradenitis suppurativa is an autoinflammatory skin disorder.They are
  difficult-to-heal and often scar. Hidradenitis suppurativa also has a
  significant psychological impact, and many patients suffer depression,
  anxiety, and impaired body-image. A hallmark of hidradenitis suppurativa
  is increased levels of biomarkers, which are molecules found in blood,
  other body fluids, or tissues that can be a sign of a normal or abnormal
  process. In hidradenitis suppurativa, these biomarkers are called
  "cytokines" and their job is to trigger the disease and make it worse.
