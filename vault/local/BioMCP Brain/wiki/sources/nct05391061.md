## Summary
The purpose of this non-interventional study is to learn about the safety and effectivness of Cibinqo Tablet for the possible treatment of atopic dermatitis (AD). AD is a long-lasting itchy red rash, caused by a skin reaction. This study is seeking participants who: 1. Are patients with moderate to severe AD who have been waiting to start treatment with Cibinqo 2.
