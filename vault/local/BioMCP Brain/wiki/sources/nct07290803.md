## Summary
The objectives of this prospective non-interventional study are to characterize the existing unmet needs across the spectrum of atopic dermatitis (AD), enhance the understanding of the patient journey, and evaluate the safety and clinical outcomes of systemic AD treatments in a real-world setting.
