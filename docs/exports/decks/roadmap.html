WEDGE FEATURE · WK 3
Horizon 01 Wedge feature

The Board Pack Drafter is the demo-to-pilot hook.

WHAT IT DOES
  • Pulls from active client's hot.md, recent meeting notes, latest P&L snapshot
  • Drafts with cited sources — per-claim wikilink-style references
  • Lands in approval queue with full preview + 5-minute countdown
  • Renders engagement-end PDF on demand
WHY IT MATTERS

Buyer's #1 fear is the cross-client send disaster. This is the feature that converts the demo to a pilot.

Without this feature, Kairos is "another note app with AI." With it, Kairos is "the thing that drafts my March board pack without crossing wires."
